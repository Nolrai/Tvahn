O King, see Achra, in the first unraveling of light

Continent of monster-gods. A roaring wilderness of vines, ruled by deathless folk of the green passages, of limitless contempt.
Dreadful Banrion.

So it is said! History is the quickening of blood, the collapse of blood, a vast and vile exhaustion.

May your fist close on it all, may it be the final closing, O King of broken light...
