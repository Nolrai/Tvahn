You’re asking for **semantic slots**, not words — a core lexeme inventory for PPSA at the stage just before perfected pig + fish farming. So:

* settled or semi-settled
* fishing is important
* pigs exist but breeding intensification is just emerging
* compound nouns common
* SVO grammar
* no heavy state bureaucracy yet
* rich distal marking system

So we want a lexicon that reflects:

* subsistence
* kin + household
* movement + place
* material culture
* early ritual
* social obligations
* weather + water
* proto-agricultural cycles

Not abstract philosophy yet.

## Why this list fits “just before perfected pig & fish farming”

* Nets, traps, enclosures, storing, and smoking exist.
* But no advanced irrigation, no markets, no coinage.
* Kinship and obligation matter more than formal state structure.
* Weather and land cycles are crucial.
* Ritual is present but still concrete.
