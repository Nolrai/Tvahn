## Linguistic Periodization

* **PPSA / Orinoco Horizon (~4000-3000 BID):**
  Earlier related speech varieties before full homogenization. Important for design reconstruction, but not recoverable in-universe as a single stable language.

* **PSA Proper (~3000-2000? BID):**
  The reconstructible common ancestor. This corresponds to broad regional linguistic and material-cultural commonality after the emergence of cities, before major daughter-language divergence.

* **Post-PSA / Early Daughter Continuum (~2000-400 BID):**
  PSA-descended varieties spread and differentiate, including the ancestors of Sahumãk. Southern traditions expand northward into Panama.

* **Late PSA / Tar-Bird Horizon (~400 BID-50 AID):**
  Better described as late PSA-descended or pre-Classical Sahumãk cultural context, unless you specifically want PSA itself to remain relatively unified this late.

* **Classical Sahumãk (~0-370 AID):**
  Attested written daughter language. Sudden durable inscriptions likely reflect transfer from older textile literacy, not invention of writing.

  ## Misc. Notes for reorganization later.

  PSA is an externally a priori language: it is not descended from or modeled on any real-world language family. Internally, however, it is treated as a reconstructed proto-language, comparable in cultural importance to Proto-Indo-European before the Age of Sail. It is the reconstructed ancestor of several languages spoken by the Catgirls of southern Middle America and northern South America. One known daughter language, Sahumank, will serve as an internal reference point for future reconstruction and family design.
  The setting is technically alternate history, but more accurately alternate natural history. The main divergence occurs in the Mesozoic, after monotremes and therian mammals split. The dominant later mammalian branch evolved a staged reproductive system tying nursing, fertility, and male gamete production together, meaning reproduction cannot occur through a lone mother. This biology strongly shapes PSA’s grammar, especially its process-based noun classes: Bearing, Traveling, and Nursing.
  Tvahn (an modern era name for the world) is wetter and cooler than Earth, with smaller deserts and larger forests. PSA speakers live in a riverine, forested region where fishing, wetland animals, early pig-analog management, and household obligation are central. The language is reconstructed for the period just before a major expansion, likely connected to improved fish and pig farming, river travel, and growing inter-household ritual networks.
  Two sapient species share this world. Their relationship resembles a caste-like social arrangement, though it is less directly exploitative than comparable human systems because their evolved social rewards encourage interspecies cooperation more than direct competition. The smaller A. minorus often appears uncomfortably subservient to American human observers, and this relationship may influence PSA’s deixis, address systems, obligation vocabulary, and social registers. Comunities have been mixed since predating A. minor's develoupment of speach, so usually any variation between speices is closer to class based "dialects" or "private languages" among an extended family then true seperate languages, and even that level of difference is almost always a skew in 'register' use rather than a difference in capabilities. That is there is bigger variation between households (which are usually fairly large by human standards, 5 is the minimum realistic size, and >10 is more normal. They don't really pair bond the way we do, instead a given group is generaly fairly promiscous with sex being more about social status and comfort then either an pair bond or herem like system. This varies less then it does in humans, but this should still be considered just the standard base line, the way a small extended families is the human default.)
  PSA speakers are short-muzzled mammals with carnivore-descended dentition, but their lips, tongue, and jaw control support broadly humanlike articulation. Their front teeth and lips permit bilabials and rounded vowels, while their non-primate tooth pattern makes labiodentals and dental fricatives rare or absent. PSA therefore favors bilabials, alveolar/postalveolar stops and affricates, velars, nasals, liquids, glides, /s h/, and glottal contrasts rather than fine dental or labiodental contrasts.
