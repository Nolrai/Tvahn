
# Context

The Golden Empire Language is a sister language to Sahumank, desended from the same PSA root, but with different sound changes, and an wildly different writing system.

The GE writing system is fundamentally first a system of mystisism, categorization, theology, and authority, and only secondarily a method of recording of speach.

This is most clear in that the placement in the semantic system is primary, and the asignment of a set of symbols to a spoken word secondary, so that its not quite a logography in the sense of classicla chinese, but rather more method of speicifing categorsations of the slots in a phrase, and ways to connect them. With a method of then pronouncing them.

But arbitrary sentances in the spoken language can't just be recorded into the writen language, and then read off. Its closer to needing to be translated into a 'standard form', encoded, and then read off, with much of the exact word choice or accent lost.

The two qualities, Light and Dark. The three genders: absorbing, given, giving. The 4 seasons. The 12 months. The 6 planets. 7 stances.

All of reality is said to be categorized by its place in the zodiac of dictionaries.
