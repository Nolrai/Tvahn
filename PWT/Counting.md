Where sets of 12 use the LONG class counter, hence the rolip hut and makrat-rol hut ect. Conventionally the Colective is used despite the fact that for many other LONG class objects the paucul would be used for groups upto 11.

However bare numbers are rare: used mostly for emphasis or to talk about numerals. When actually counting or talking about sets of objects, counters are used, that are also used as pronouns. Technically my "COLL" is a plural but its confusing to speakers of singular/plural marked languages (like English) so I use COLL for collective instead.
